Terms and Conditions

1. Booking Policy

All bookings require a 25% down payment to confirm your reservation. The remaining balance must be paid upon arrival or service completion.

2. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made at least 24 hours before your appointment will receive a full refund. Cancellations made within 24 hours may forfeit the down payment.

3. Late Arrival

Please arrive 10 minutes before your scheduled appointment. Late arrivals may result in reduced service time to accommodate other clients.

4. Health Conditions

Please inform us of any health conditions, allergies, or injuries before your service. Certain conditions may require medical clearance.

5. Privacy Policy

Your personal information will be kept confidential and used only for booking and service purposes.

6. Therapist Assignment

If you select "Any Available Therapist", our admin will assign a qualified therapist based on availability and expertise.

7. Payment Terms

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD), PayPal, GCash, and Credit Card. Payment method must be selected during booking confirmation.
