Terms and Conditions

Please read the following Terms and Conditions carefully before booking an appointment through the zentraX system for Nagomi Wellness Spa. By proceeding with your booking, you agree to comply with the policies stated below.

1. Booking Summary

Please review and double-check your selected service, number of client/s, preferred therapist/s, date, time, and personal information before confirming your booking. You will be updated once your booking has been approved or updated through the Booking ID (policy #2).

2. Booking ID

A Booking ID is automatically generated for every booking request submitted through the system. Please keep your Booking ID to track booking updates and manage your appointment.

To check your booking details:

  1. Go to Home
  2. Click Manage Booking
  3. Select Transaction ID
  4. Enter your Booking ID

If you forgot your Booking ID, you may search your booking by entering the Name and Phone Number used during the booking process.

3. Rescheduling or Cancelling a Booking

Clients may reschedule or cancel their booking in case of emergencies, schedule changes, or therapist/s unavailability.

To reschedule or cancel your booking:

  1. Go to Home
  2. Click Manage Booking
  3. Enter your Booking ID or Name and Phone Number
  4. Below the booking details, you will find the Reschedule and Cancel options.

Rescheduling Policy

  • You may only modify the date and time of your appointment.
  • Each booking is allowed only one (1) reschedule request to prevent scheduling conflicts.
  • Rescheduling is subject to therapist availability and spa schedule.

Cancellation Policy

  • If you cancel your booking, you will be asked to provide the reason for cancellation.
  • Cancellation should be done 1–2 hours before the scheduled appointment time to avoid inconvenience to other clients.

4. Booking Appointment Day

  • Clients are advised to arrive 10–15 minutes before their scheduled appointment time.
  • Late arrivals may result in shortened service time to accommodate other scheduled clients.
  • Late clients who booked 1 hour are only given 10-15 minutes to arrive. Failure to show up will result in cancellation.
  • Late clients who booked 1.5 hour or 2 hours are only given 15–20 minutes to arrive. Failure to show up will result in cancellation.
  • Service extensions are not allowed if they conflict with the next booking schedule.

5. Therapist Availability

While the spa strives to provide your preferred therapist, assignments may change due to unforeseen circumstances. The spa will assign another qualified therapist to ensure your service proceeds as scheduled.

If your chosen therapist is unavailable and you don't want another therapist, you may reschedule (policy #3).

6. Health Conditions

Clients are required to inform the spa of any health conditions, allergies, injuries, or medical concerns before receiving any service. The spa reserves the right to decline or adjust services if a condition may pose health risks.

7. Payment Methods

  • Cash
  • GCash
  • Maya
  • Credit/Debit Card

All payments are collected at the spa after the service has been completed.

8. Feedback and Reviews

Clients are encouraged to leave a review after their appointment.

  1. Go to Home
  2. Click Reviews at the upper corner of the page
  3. Select Write a Review
  4. Fill in the required information and submit

Please ensure all reviews are honest, respectful, and appropriate, as they are publicly displayed on the homepage.

9. Privacy Policy

All personal information collected through the zentraX booking system will remain strictly confidential. Nagomi Wellness Spa will not share, sell, or disclose personal information to third parties without the client's consent.

10. Agreement to Terms

By submitting a booking through the zentraX system, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms and Conditions.
