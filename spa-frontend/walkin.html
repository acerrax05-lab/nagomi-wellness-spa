Terms and Conditions

Please read the following Terms and Conditions carefully before booking. By proceeding, you agree to comply with the policies stated below.

1. Booking Summary

Please review and double-check your selected service, number of clients, preferred therapist, date, time, and personal information before confirming.

2. Booking ID

A Booking ID is automatically generated for every booking. Please keep it to track updates and manage your appointment via Manage Booking.

3. Rescheduling or Cancelling

Each booking allows one (1) reschedule. Cancellation should be done 1–2 hours before the appointment to avoid inconvenience.

4. Appointment Day

  • Arrive 10–15 minutes before your appointment.
  • Clients who arrive more than 30 minutes late may have their session cancelled.

5. Therapist Availability

The spa may assign a different qualified therapist if your preferred one is unavailable.

6. Health Conditions

Please inform the spa of any health conditions, allergies, or medical concerns before receiving any service.

7. Payment

Cash, GCash, Maya, and Credit/Debit Card. All payments are collected after the service.

8. Privacy

All personal information collected is strictly confidential and will not be shared with third parties.
